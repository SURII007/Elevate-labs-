PHISHING EMAIL SAMPLE ANALYZED:

Subject: Urgent: Your Bank Account Will Be Locked!
Sender: support@hdfcbank-secure-alert.com

Email Body:
Dear Customer,

Your account has been temporarily suspended due to suspicious activity.
Verify your identity immediately to avoid permanent account closure.

Click Here to Verify

Failure to verify within 2 hours will result in account termination.

Regards,
HDFC Security Team

--------------------------------------------

PHISHING INDICATORS IDENTIFIED:

1. Fake Sender Address (Email Spoofing)
The email claims to be from HDFC Bank, but the sender’s domain
"hdfcbank-secure-alert.com" is fake. Official HDFC emails only come from
"hdfcbank.com". This confirms email spoofing.

2. Urgent and Threatening Language
The attacker creates fear using phrases such as:
- "Account will be locked"
- "Verify within 2 hours"
- "Permanent closure"
This forces the victim to act without thinking.

3. Suspicious and Mismatched Link
The email displays "Click Here to Verify" but the real URL redirects to:
http://hdfc-login-secure.tk/verify
This is a non-bank and unsafe domain.

4. No Personal Identification
The email starts with "Dear Customer" instead of using the customer’s real name,
which legitimate banks always use.

5. Grammar and Formatting Errors
The email contains poor sentence structure and unprofessional layout, which is
a common sign of phishing.

6. Email Header Discrepancies
Using an online header analyzer, the following issues were found:
- Foreign IP address detected
- SPF authentication failed
- DKIM authentication failed
This shows the email was sent from a fake server.

7. Use of Social Engineering Techniques
The attacker uses:
- Fear
- Urgency
- Authority impersonation (Bank)

INTERVIEW QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS:

1. What is phishing?
Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers trick users into giving their
personal or financial information using fake emails or websites.

2. How do you identify a phishing email?
By checking suspicious sender address, urgent language, spelling mistakes,
unknown links, and email headers.

3. What is email spoofing?
Email spoofing is when attackers fake the sender’s address to make the email
appear legitimate.

4. Why are phishing emails dangerous?
They can steal passwords, bank details, and personal identity, causing financial
loss and data breaches.

5. How can you verify the sender’s authenticity?
By checking the domain name, email headers, SPF/DKIM records, and official
company website.

6. What tools can analyze email headers?
MXToolbox, Google Admin Toolbox, Mail Header Analyzer.

7. What actions should be taken on phishing emails?
- Do not click any links
- Do not open attachments
- Report the email as spam
- Delete immediately

8. How do attackers use social engineering in phishing?
They manipulate emotions like fear, urgency, trust, and authority to trick
victims.
